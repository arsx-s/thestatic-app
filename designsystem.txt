6. Atmosphere layers
Scroll video: one video across the whole page. Frame one is at the top and the last frame is at the very bottom. It is greyscale with contrast 1.15, under the shade overlay. It re-encodes as all-intra at 480px wide and 15fps so scrubbing stays smooth. It cannot be seen at all if the video fails, and the page still works.
Scan lines: a 1px line every 4px at black .35, with the layer at .5 opacity.
Grain: monochrome fractal noise at .08 opacity, jittering in 4 steps over .5s.
Reduced motion: grain and marquee stop, reveals appear instantly, scroll is not scrubbed, and transitions shrink to near zero.
7. Components

Primary button (BECOME A STATIC)

Pill, minimum height 56px, padding 0 2rem.
Ink fill, black text, Space Grotesk 700, 1rem, tracking .06em.
Shadow: a 1px white ring plus a solid 5px offset in 
#4a4a4a.
Hover: a flat translucent strip skewed -24° slides across.
Press: moves 3px down and right.

Secondary button: same shape with a transparent fill, ink text and an ink border.

Dock

Fixed at the bottom centre. It has 5 destinations: Home, Frequencies, Sessions, Manifesto, Join.
Items are 48px with a 22px stroke icon (stroke 2, rounded caps).
Inactive items sit at .5 opacity and active ones at 1, with a small dot under the active item.
Magnification is distance-based: 1.32× scale, 8px lift, 2 neighbours either side, cosine falloff.
Hover and focus show a label pill.
The current section is marked with aria-current="location".

Player card (NowPlaying)

Fixed top right. 62px high, opens to 132px. Width is 250px, or the viewport minus 92px on narrow screens.
Cover is a 44px square with the logo inverted. The play button is a 44px ink circle.
Title is Space Grotesk and artist is mono. A 2px progress rail runs along the bottom.
Pause and volume are the only controls.

Volume slider (WakeSlider)

26 thin bars, 14px high, 4px wide at most. Lit bars are ink and unlit bars are 
#3a3a3a.
Dragging fast makes the bars you pass spring up to 2.6× and settle. Energy decays by exp(-6.5·dt).
Keyboard: arrows move by 1, Shift+arrows by 10, Home and End jump to the ends.

Frequency rows

Full-width buttons with a mono index (FREQ./001) and a large display title.
Hover and focus invert the row to an ink fill with black text, add 1.2rem of left padding and reveal “ADD TO MY APPLICATION ↗”.
A selected row gets a 6px left border.

Chips

44px minimum height, pill shape, 
#3a3a3a border.
Checked chips invert to ink fill with black text.
Focus shows a 2px ink outline.

Form fields

Fill #000, border 
#3a3a3a, radius 10px, minimum height 48px.
Focus: ink border plus a 2px black and 2px ink ring.
Error: dashed white border and a mono message under the field.
Required and optional status is written in each label.

Status message: 3px white left border with 1rem of left padding.

Gooey loader: three 16px ink dots on a 1.5s ease-in-out loop, ±14px travel, 0.2s stagger, with an SVG goo filter (blur 3, alpha matrix 18 / -7). It appears only during real waits, such as video load and form submit.

Pixel handshake: a 64×40 canvas drawn as a 32×20 grid of 2px cells, showing hands on a feature-phone frame. Pixels fall and fade as you scroll through the first 75% of the viewport height.

HeatMap word: “THE STATIC” drawn blurred into a field, 720×200. Grey at rest. The cursor cools the field into greyscale contour bands. It is decorative and hidden from screen readers.

Instagram link: a pill with a 22px outlined icon and “FOLLOW @THESTATIC.CTG ON INSTAGRAM” in mono. It inverts to ink on hover.

8. Motion
Easing curves: cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) for arrivals (reveals, player morph, CTA strip). cubic-bezier(.24, 1.34, .38, 1) for the dock’s overshoot.
Durations: 150–220ms for hover, press and colour. 420–460ms for morphs. 700ms for scroll reveals, which start 24px low and fade in.
Scroll video smoothing: moves 22% of the remaining distance each frame.
Rule: no motion or sound that isn’t doing a job. No hover sounds.
9. Accessibility rules
Focus ring: 2px ink outline with 3px offset.
Minimum touch target: 44px.
Skip link, semantic landmarks, labelled forms, and live regions for errors and status.
Every animated layer has a reduced-motion fallback.
Text on dark keeps strong contrast. The mid grey (
#8b8b8b) is for labels only, never long text.
